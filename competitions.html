Competitions

The Delftsche Dodgers will be participating in its fourth competition season in the 2026-2027 season run by Dodgeball Bond Nederland (DBN). There are multiple competition days spread out throughout the season (around 1 every month) in which teams from all over the country play matches against each other. At the Join Us tab you can find all the pricing regarding the membership and the costs of joining the competition. Learn more about the Dutch national league at the DBN’s website: https://dodgeballbondnederland.nl/index.php
